# Fixed-Income Guidance

- Follow the repository root guidance and `{{CLAUDE_ROOT}}\MD\standards\DATA_ACCESS.md`.
- Route Korean Treasury Bond work to `ktb\CLAUDE.md`.
- Route U.S. Treasury security and curve work to `ust\CLAUDE.md`.
- Read `market-data\CLAUDE.md` before using New York Fed Markets Data or Databento.
- Preserve instrument identifiers, issuer, currency, maturity, coupon, price or yield definition, day-count and compounding conventions when relevant, observation timestamp, time zone, source, and retrieval date.
- Do not combine yields, prices, par rates, reference rates, or fitted curves without making the conceptual differences explicit.
