# Fiscal Data Guidance

- Follow the repository root guidance and `{{CLAUDE_ROOT}}\MD\standards\DATA_ACCESS.md`.
- Route Treasury Fiscal Data API work to `treasury\CLAUDE.md`.
- Route legacy or programme-specific Bureau of the Fiscal Service downloads to `fiscal-service\CLAUDE.md`.
- Route White House OMB and Congressional Budget Office work to `omb-cbo\CLAUDE.md`.
- Route Treasury International Capital work to `tic\CLAUDE.md`.
- Route Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee and Quarterly Refunding materials to `tbac\CLAUDE.md`.
- Preserve the fiscal year, reference period, publication vintage, revision status, units, table or series identifier, source URL, and retrieval date.
