# Registered Federal Reserve Surveys, Inflation, and Expectations Data

- Use the registered aliases `ATLANTA_FED_WAGE_TRACKER`, `ATLANTA_FED_STICKY_PRICE_CPI`, `RICHMOND_FED_MANUFACTURING_SURVEY`, `RICHMOND_FED_NONMANUFACTURING_SURVEY`, `DALLAS_FED_TMOS_TSSOS`, `KANSAS_CITY_FED_MANUFACTURING_SURVEY`, `PHILADELPHIA_FED_NBOS`, `NEW_YORK_FED_EMPIRE_STATE_SURVEY`, `SAN_FRANCISCO_FED_PCE_INFLATION`, `CLEVELAND_FED_INFLATION_DATA`, `DALLAS_FED_TRIMMED_MEAN_PCE`, and `NEW_YORK_FED_SCE` only through their registered service definitions and approved helper.
- Treat `NEW_YORK_FED_EMPIRE_STATE_SURVEY` as survey downloads separate from `NEW_YORK_FED_MARKETS`. For Dallas Fed, distinguish TMOS manufacturing data from TSSOS service-sector data and preserve SA versus NSA status.
- Use `RICHMOND_FED_NONMANUFACTURING_SURVEY` (registered 2026-09-15 under the standing approval for official public sources) for the Fifth District non-manufacturing (service sector) survey history workbook `nmf_historicaldata.xlsx`. Keep it separate from the manufacturing survey and preserve the NSA and SA column pairs; `sa_svc_emp_e` is the seasonally adjusted expected-employment diffusion index.
- Use `SAN_FRANCISCO_FED_PCE_INFLATION` for Supply- and Demand-Driven PCE and Cyclical and Acyclical Core PCE data. Preserve the decomposition, headline-versus-core definition, observation month, source URL, and retrieval date.
- Use `ATLANTA_FED_STICKY_PRICE_CPI` for Flexible CPI, Core Flexible CPI, Sticky CPI, Core Sticky CPI, Sticky CPI excluding shelter, and Core Sticky CPI excluding shelter. For nonannualized month-over-month rates, multiply the workbook's monthly decimal change by 100; use the workbook's 12-month columns directly for year-over-year rates. Preserve the observation month, source URL, retrieval date, and any revised history.
- Use `CLEVELAND_FED_INFLATION_DATA` for Median CPI, 16% Trimmed Mean CPI, Median PCE, and Inflation Nowcasting. Match month-over-month, year-over-year, or quarterly data to the requested comparison, and identify nowcasts as estimates that may change daily rather than official realised inflation.
- Use `DALLAS_FED_TRIMMED_MEAN_PCE` for item-level price-change distributions and Trimmed Mean PCE. Treat `pcehist.xlsx` or an existing validated local history as the historical base. Because `pcedata.xlsx` contains only the latest 13 months, never use it to replace older history; merge by observation month, update matching recent months only after checking definitions, append new months, and preserve all older observations.
- Use `NEW_YORK_FED_SCE` for the Survey of Consumer Expectations Chart Data workbook and Data Bank. Keep it separate from `NEW_YORK_FED_MARKETS` and `NEW_YORK_FED_EMPIRE_STATE_SURVEY`; preserve survey wave, population, statistic, units, and retrieval or revision date.
- These registrations do not include the University of Michigan consumer survey or Conference Board subscription data. Do not collect either source unless the user separately authorises and configures it.
- Run `scripts\Test-RegisteredKeylessDataSources.ps1 -ServiceAlias <ALIAS>` for a small connection check when setup or availability is in doubt.

## Federal Reserve Balance Sheet (H.4.1) and FOMC calendar

- Use `FEDERAL_RESERVE_H41` (registered 2026-09-15 under the standing approval for official public sources) for the H.4.1 Factors Affecting Reserve Balances release: the Data Download Program full XML package `FRB_h41_xml.zip` and `releaseDates.json` under `/releases/h41/`. Values in the XML are in millions of U.S. dollars; series ending in `_N.WW` are Wednesday levels and `_N.WA` weekly averages. Never mix the two, and never assume a Thursday release date; read the actual date from `releaseDates.json`.
- Preserve the series name, Wednesday observation date, release date, observation status (`A`), unit, source URL, and retrieval date. Derived items (other loans, other absorption, weighted average maturity from maturity buckets, reserves-to-GDP) must be labelled as calculated.
- Use `FEDERAL_RESERVE_SEP` for the FOMC calendar page when a task needs meeting dates (for example the quarter-month meeting start that rolls federal funds futures contracts). Do not guess meeting dates; fall back to a cached calendar only when the page is unavailable and say so.
