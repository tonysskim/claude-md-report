# Macroeconomic Data Guidance

- Follow the repository root guidance and `{{CLAUDE_ROOT}}\MD\standards\DATA_ACCESS.md`.
- Route Korean macroeconomic work to `korea\CLAUDE.md` and then to the relevant provider folder.
- Route U.S. macroeconomic, labour, demographic, housing, and regional Federal Reserve work to `us\CLAUDE.md` and then to the relevant provider folder.
- Route international macroeconomic work to `international\CLAUDE.md` and then to the relevant provider folder.
- Preserve the series identifier, frequency, unit, seasonal-adjustment status, geography, observation period, release or vintage, revision status, source URL, and retrieval date.
- Never silently substitute a similar indicator when the requested concept is unavailable.
